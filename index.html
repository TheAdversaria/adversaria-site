About the Keeper

This is where the drafts live before they're ready to stop being drafts. I'm not sure anything is ever fully ready to stop being one — finished always felt like a decision made under deadline more than a fact anyone discovered.

I write slowly, keep too much, publish less than I keep. The margins are usually where the truer version is. Not always. There's one page where the margin note is worse than the draft, and I left it in anyway, because taking it out felt like lying about how the thing got made.

I don't fully know what unfinished means anymore. I use the word constantly.
